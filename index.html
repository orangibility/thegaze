About

PERSON / 001

I like looking at the things most people walk past.

This site is a personal space for collecting the things that catch my attention.

Photography, graffiti, music, underground culture, places, people and ideas.

There is no particular order to it. The archive is supposed to feel like walking through a city at night — you turn a corner without knowing what you'll find.
